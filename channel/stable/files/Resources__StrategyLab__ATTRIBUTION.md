# Strategy Lab asset sources

The Visual Strategy Editor 0.4 does **not** download map images or tower portraits from websites. Its map canvas comes from a local Roblox screenshot captured by Ultimate Macro (or a screenshot selected manually by the user), and its placement circles/badges are generated by Strategy Lab itself.

The separate RewardLibrary can optionally fetch reward/drop reference icons from the Tower Defense Simulator Wiki through its MediaWiki API and cache small local thumbnails. Reward image retrieval is cosmetic and is not part of the Editor render loop or gameplay timing.

Tower Defense Simulator and its game artwork are owned by their respective rights holders. Wiki/community reward media may carry separate attribution/license requirements.
